         Word Builders: Prefixes & Suffixes (un-, re-, -less, -ment)
a. Read and Learn
              Prefix: a group of letters added to the beginning of a word to
               change its meaning. (un + happy = unhappy)
              Suffix: a group of letters added to the end of a word to change its
               form or meaning. (help + ful = helpful)
             Examples:
   Base Word      Form             Affix       New Word    Form     Meaning of New Word
  healthy      adjective      prefix (un-)    unhealthy   adjective not healthy
  play         verb           prefix (re-)    replay      verb      play again
  care         verb/ noun suffix (-less)      careless    adjective without care
  move         verb           suffix (-ment) movement     noun      the act of moving
