          Words That Go Together: Stay Safe with Collocations!

 What are collocations?
 Collocations are words that often go together.
 For example, we say:
   • make a mistake ✔          not (do a mistake ✖)
   • stay safe ✔               not (be safe ✖)
   • follow instructions ✔ not (obey instructions ✖)
 We use collocations to sound natural in English.

Safety Collocations – Learn & Remember

           avoid                   5. announcements, warnings
